**DASHBOARD STRUCTURE (6 TABS):**

1. **Home – Daily Alignment Hub**
   - Greeting from the AI guide
   - Daily mantra or message
   - “Life Balance Wheel” snapshot
   - Suggestion for one area to work on today

2. **Trackers (The 5 P.A.P.H. Dimensions)**
   - **Physical:** Habits, sleep, water, food, exercise
   - **Emotional:** Mood check-ins, stress levels, venting voice memos
   - **Intellectual:** Learning goals, reading log, reflection journal
   - **Spiritual:** Meditation, prayer log, synchronicities, dreams
   - **Relational:** Relationships, gratitude, boundaries, social balance

   *Each dimension has its own subtab, progress tracker, chart, and AI feedback system.*

3. **Guided Consultations**
   - Choose: Physical / Emotional / Intellectual / Spiritual / Relational
   - Open-ended reflection with AI support
   - Gemini prompts + suggestions + visualizations
   - Custom rituals, breathing, exercises
   - Audio + video guided sessions (YouTube integrations)

4. **Goals & Rituals**
   - Short-term / long-term goals across dimensions
   - Daily/Weekly ritual planner
   - Progress rings + reward system
   - Sync with calendar (reminders, sessions, sessions logged)

5. **Archive & Timeline**
   - Timeline of self-growth: entries, breakthroughs, emotional states
   - Searchable emotional tags
   - Journaling archive with TTS (can listen to past entries)

6. **Settings & Sync**
   - AI tuning: tone, gender, spiritual style (neutral / divine / stoic / intuitive)
   - Language toggle ENG/ESP
   - Backup to Google Drive
   - API integration manager
